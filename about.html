About Feida Wang

I’m Feida Wang — many people call me Bruce — a software engineer based in Melbourne. AFFLATUS is my personal studio: I design, build and ship working software with coding agents, and publish the research and writing that comes out of it.

I like problems that sit between disciplines, where an interface, a data pipeline, a financial model and a piece of writing all have to agree. Everything here is a working product rather than a mock-up, and the research pages say where their numbers come from.

Highlights

  • Built this bilingual site on my own with coding agents, from interface design and front end to data pipelines and deployment.
  • Run automated data refreshes for markets, macro events and daily astronomy, each checked before it is published.
  • Research the AI industry and US markets, keeping measurements, disclosures, calculations and hypotheses separate.
  • Wrote a 24-week bilingual course on building useful tools with AI, and serialise original novels in Chinese.

What I’m working on

  • AI Observatory

    An atlas of the companies and frontier models building AI, from agentic coding to the path to AGI.

  • Fed Watch

    Federal Reserve decisions, US fiscal policy and how they reach the AI industry, with dated primary sources.

  • Course

    A 24-week course: build useful tools, verify AI agents, then deploy, recover and improve them.

  • Stories

    Original serialised fiction in Chinese, with reading progress kept on your device.
