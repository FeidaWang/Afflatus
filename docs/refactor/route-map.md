## Locale route behavior

- Published locale prefixes are generated by `scripts/localize-site.mjs` into `dist/en/` and `dist/zh/`.
- Home locale links are `/en/` and `/zh/`; language switching is implemented by the shared brand/navigation layer and per-page locale scripts.
- Serial is intentionally Chinese-only; `/en/serial.html` redirects to `/zh/serial.html`.

## Design-spec concept mapping

The design spec names future concepts rather than current routes. M00 maps them without implementing M01+:

- Home → `/`.
- Deck/Command → current expanded deck in `/portfolio.html`; no separate `/command` route exists yet.
- Markets → current `arena` route and home market/signal surfaces.
- Lab → current `sectors` and `arena` routes.
- Writing → current `serial` and content surfaces.
- Experiments → current `boot` prototype and scene/combat experiments; no `/experiments/flight` route exists yet.
- About → home `#about` section; no separate About route exists.
