01

The frontier goes multipolar.

Both blocs light up at once. 02

Capital picks ecosystems, not isolated models.

US private AI investment runs roughly 23 times China's, partly offset by state-guided funds on the other side. Alibaba's disclosed Moonshot stake, cloud-linked frontier investments, the 77-name open-weights letter and the separate 52-partner security alliance show how capital, policy and distribution now move as bloc-level decisions.
