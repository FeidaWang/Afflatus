Accessible city summary

This generated Shanghai concept reserves a vertical waterfront and three hero blocks: a pearl mast, a stepped crown and a corn-cob curve tower. These are planning silhouettes, not surveyed buildings or GIS.

  • Drag the day slider with a pointer or keyboard.
  • Build plays 210 days in about 23 seconds.
  • Tour is optional and becomes a static overview when reduced motion is enabled.
  • During a tour, a feathered safety field raises the camera above nearby finished structures without changing its orbit direction.
  • Hero view cycles through the available concept landmarks without changing the construction day.
  • A low-poly survey helicopter follows a roof-safe orbit; reduced motion freezes it and both rotors.
  • Rendering may reduce vehicle and tree density for performance, but simulated traffic and capacity metrics always use the complete city plan.
  • The data panel is closed by default. When open, each simulated value names its construction-state inputs; when closed, metric DOM updates stop.
  • The Layers editor can hide six render categories without changing construction state or simulated metrics; it is mutually exclusive with the data panel.
  • Landmark and selected large-building cranes follow their own schedules, with no more than six active citywide; scrubbing backward reconstructs them exactly.
