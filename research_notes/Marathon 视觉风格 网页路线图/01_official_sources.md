## 问题 1：Bungie 用什么原话描述 Marathon 的美术风格？引用了哪些影响来源？

### Takeaway
Bungie 内部与对外均用 **"Graphic Realism"（图形化写实）** 一词（由 Franchise Art Director Joseph Cross 提出），核心公式是「简化的、平面设计驱动的视觉 + 有限材质」叠加「真实比例/尺度 + 暗示性的功能细节 + 落地的世界」；Bungie 自己对 Runner 外观的速记是 **"Nike meets Teenage Engineering"**；Global Franchise Director Philip Asher 另给出 **"greatest hits of '90s futurism"** 的叙事框架（Wipeout、PlayStation "Mental Wealth" 广告、透明 DualShock）。未找到任何官方来源提到 Dieter Rams 或 Y2K 一词。

### Cited Findings
- 术语："Graphic Realism"：Joseph Cross 称这是 "mainly a production-facing term, meant to frame how we think about and prioritize aspects of our visual world"；一面是 "simplified, graphic-design-driven visuals with limited materials"，另一面是 "realistic proportions & scale, implied functional & detail, and a generally grounded world"（引自 Cross 2023 年社媒长文，经 Creative Bloq 转述） — [Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/3d/video-game-design/bungies-art-director-explains-marathons-controversial-art-style)；同文另见 [inkl 转载](https://www.inkl.com/news/bungie-s-art-director-explains-marathon-s-controversial-art-style)
- Cross 列出的「游戏业内」影响：Metal Gear Solid 2、初代 Marathon、Wipeout；当代作品 Death Stranding、God of War、The Last of Us；DICE 的 Mirror's Edge — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/marathon-art-director-shares-the-team-s-creative-influences)
- Cross 列出的「非游戏」影响：电影 Aeon Flux、Ghost in the Shell；漫画家 Katsuhiro Otomo（大友克洋）、动画导演 Koji Morimoto（森本晃司）、平面设计工作室 The Designers Republic、MV 导演 Chris Cunningham；以及 "product and apparel design, photography, science, conceptual art, cinematography"，运动类 Formula 1、MotoGP 等 — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/marathon-art-director-shares-the-team-s-creative-influences)；[thegamepost](https://thegamepost.com/marathon-art-style-mirrors-edge-ghost-in-the-shell/)；[PSU](https://www.psu.com/news/marathon-takes-inspiration-from-mirrors-edge-ghost-in-the-shell-more-for-its-art-style/)
- Cross 另提到 Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse 作为影响（对动画/运动语言有参考价值） — [Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/3d/video-game-design/bungies-art-director-explains-marathons-controversial-art-style)
- Cross 的目标表述：打造 "a distinct visual 'brand' of science fiction that 'feels fresh and stands the test of time'" — [exputer](https://exputer.com/news/games/marathon-art-style-mirrors-edge/)
- 2023 年初次揭露时媒体总结为 "Mirror's Edge, Wipeout, Formula One" 组合 — [Push Square 2023-05](https://pushsquare.com/news/2023/05/marathon-channelling-mirrors-edge-wipeout-formula-one-with-striking-art-style)
- Bungie 对 Runner 的速记 **"Nike meets Teenage Engineering"**，GameSpot 转述并描述为 "high-fidelity, low-poly industrial design"、"visually busy and dense, but at the same time, easy to digest"、Runner 的细节与 flair "inspired by sports fashion" — [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/bungies-marathon-is-a-polished-extraction-shooter-but-that-might-not-be-enough/1100-6530826/)
- 德媒转述 Cross：产品与时装设计，尤其 "Hightech-Sport-Fashion von Nike"，以及 Formula 1 的刺眼信号色（"grellen Signal-Farben der Formel 1"）大量出现在 Marathon 中；视觉主题被概括为 "Cyberpunk meets Formula 1" — [PC-Welt](https://www.pcwelt.de/?p=2766948)
- Kotaku 称视觉识别借鉴范围 "as wide-ranging as Nike sneaker culture and Ikea furniture assembly" — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/marathon-bungie-development-runner-art-2000661234)；VGTimes 标题亦提 IKEA 风格 — [VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/146538-the-greatest-hits-of-90s-futurism-bungie-reveals-what-inspired-the-creation-of-marathon.html)
