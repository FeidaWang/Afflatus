# City data ledger

`city-data-ledger.json` is the fail-closed source and release registry for Shanghai, Melbourne and Hong Kong. It is evidence about candidate data, not a licence grant and not a runtime CityPackage.

`melbourne-p0-licence-evidence-2026-08-15.json` is a normalized snapshot of five official dataset records plus the controlling DataVic and CC BY pages. Its SHA-256 is pinned by the corresponding ledger entries and checked by CI. It is discovery evidence only: it does not archive the selected GIS download, download-time terms, CRS metadata or a legal decision.

`melbourne-control-dem-licence-evidence-2026-08-16.json` similarly pins the official Vicmap Position and Vicmap Elevation DEM 10m records, DataShare terms, MetaShare metadata and CC BY terms. The signed review permits bounded acquisition and engineering processing only; it is not production approval.

`city-package-registry.json` is the only production CityPackage allow-list. Its three entries remain `null` until a local manifest, every source layer in the ledger, spatial QA and all four approval roles pass independently. Adding files below `public/assets/city/packages/` does not make them publishable.

The building, road, pedestrian, Hydro, Urban Forest tree and Vicmap survey-control inventory records are approved raw acquisitions. Their source GeoJSON/ZIP and HTTP headers stay below ignored `data/city/raw/`; committed inventories, QA reports and small engineering fixtures preserve checksums and reproducibility without publishing the full source responses. `reviews/2026-08-15-melbourne-pedestrian-source-strategy.md` records why the complete pedestrian ZIP is primary and why the portal v2 geometry-only export is only Plan B. The Hydro inventory preserves the exact official Water Area and Water Structure Line FeatureServer responses as two members of one immutable ZIP. The survey-control fixture keeps 40 status-OK, GDA2020-adjusted and AHD spirit-levelled marks; it treats the published GDA2020 DMS/MGA2020 pair as authoritative and explicitly classifies mixed datum behaviour in the service geometry.

## Rules

1. Do not place raw GIS, imagery, point clouds, 3D tiles or provider responses in `public/` or Git from a `review`, `blocked` or `rejected` entry.
2. A layer may enter the isolated raw inventory only after `decisions.acquisition` is `approved`, the licence snapshot has a SHA-256 hash, caching is allowed, all rights are resolved, and both `dataOwner` and `legal` have named, dated evidence.
3. A layer may enter a production CityPackage only after the precinct, tile inventory and control points are frozen; spatial metadata and the source artifact checksum are verified; every right is `allowed`; all four approvals are complete; and every city release blocker is resolved.
4. A portal label such as “open data”, “CC BY” or “ODbL” is discovery evidence only. Save the actual dataset-record and download-time terms before approving acquisition.
5. Data licensing and public-map compliance are separate decisions. This is especially important for Shanghai.
6. Hong Kong TPB statutory planning data remains blocked until prior written authorisation is archived; the general CSDI terms do not override its specific restriction.
7. A CityPackage must use local, immutable, checksummed assets. Runtime remote GIS URLs are not accepted as production package assets.

## Updating a candidate

- Preserve the old raw artifact and licence snapshot; a new upstream release gets a new immutable inventory record.
- Record the exact dataset version, capture/update date, source CRS, vertical datum and SHA-256.
- Resolve ambiguous labels such as `MGA55`, `WGS84` or `resource-specific` from the downloaded file metadata.
- Add named approval evidence rather than changing a status alone.
- Do not edit an immutable evidence bundle in place. Add a dated replacement, update the ledger reference and preserve the superseded file.
- Run `npm run data:check` and `npm test` before committing.
