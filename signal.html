Sources & methodology

A transparent record of what we know—and when.

01 / Primary evidence

Federal Reserve and New York Fed releases establish monetary policy. Treasury, IRS, OMB and White House documents establish fiscal and industrial measures. BEA and BLS provide economic context.

02 / Facts and interpretation

A speech expresses its speaker’s view. A proposal still needs enactment. AI transmission notes are conditional editorial analysis, not observed returns or committee forecasts.

03 / Coverage and updates

Reviewed weekly on Monday at 09:00 Australia/Melbourne. The checked date reflects source review; each event retains its publication date.
