## Venator Class Star Destroyer

- Creator: [ForkyForklift](https://sketchfab.com/ForkyForklift)
- Source: [Venator Class Star Destroyer](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/venator-class-star-destroyer-ff65cd3c27234615a3b68088f67e99e4)
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Changes: converted from Sketchfab's 1K GLB download; geometry simplified to a 20% web LOD, flattened, pruned and Meshopt-encoded; textures resized to 512px and converted to mipmapped KTX2; model recentered and normalized at runtime. The transparent `venator-hero-poster.webp` is a static render of this optimized asset for reduced-motion, low-tier and loading states.
